Proposal for ZimPost · October 2026

Fair parcel prices that keep ZimPost's revenue

Price by distance, size and speed, as DHL, UPS, FedEx and China's couriers do, adapted to ZimPost's network. On a stand-in month of 6,000 shipments the new prices earn 3.1% to 4.8% more than today's charts, whatever speed customers choose. Short trips get cheaper, long trips cost a little more, and extras like door delivery are new income on top.

Why change

What today's charts get wrong

  • Zones are a town list, not distance. 127 km to Chinhoyi costs $7, more than 447 km to Bulawayo at $5. Norton costs the same as Beitbridge.
  • Only weight counts. A big, light box pays like a small one, though it fills the van.
  • The EMS bands jump, 17 kg is missing and 45 kg sits in two bands.
  • The medicine rate isn't cheaper. 30 kg of medicine costs $35, against $25 on EMS.
  • EMS and Fast Parcel cost the same, so speed has no price and the slower service has no reason to exist.
  • No charges for extras: door collection or delivery, remote places, oversize, cover, cash on delivery.
  • Fuel is baked into the charts, so every diesel change means new charts or a thinner margin.
The method

How a price is worked out

1. What is it?

Every quote starts with the item. A cellphone has one secure price, $5 before VAT, any weight; the battery stays inside, at most two phones a parcel, and the value is declared for cover. Medicine goes priority at Express speed for about today's price (+3% on average, cheaper when heavy), sealed and labelled in English. Fresh food goes Express only. Not accepted: cash, jewellery and precious stones, weapons, explosives, power banks and loose batteries.

2. Chargeable weight

The real weight, or the size weight (L × W × H in cm ÷ 6,000, as SF Express and JD use), whichever is more, rounded up to 0.5 kg. Size only counts for pieces of 0.05 m³ or more (about 40 × 40 × 31 cm), so small items always go by weight. In year one size can count for at most double the real weight, for boxes up to 0.25 m³; anything bigger pays its full size. No side may be over 150 cm for a parcel; longer goes as freight.

3. Zone by road distance

Same town ×0.8 · up to 120 km ×0.9 · up to 250 km ×0.95 · up to 450 km ×1 (today's Zone A level) · up to 650 km ×1.08 · beyond ×1.15.

4. Service

Standard (Fast Parcel) is today's price. Express (EMS), next day by 12:00 between main centres, is +15%. In town a motorbike same-day service is offered too. Economy, collected at the post office in 3 to 5 days, is −10% and fills spare space. The EMS Pack stays at its flat national price.

5. Freight and whole vehicles

From 71 kg, freight by the kg, cheaper per kg as loads grow, with an 80 kg minimum so 71 kg never costs a quarter more than 70 kg. A whole vehicle (bike to 30 t), priced from fuel, wear, driver days, tolls and the trip back plus 25%, is offered from 3,000 kg or when freight can't take the load, and never for less than the largest freight load on that route, so 3,000 kg never costs less than 2,999 kg.

6. Fuel, extras, VAT

Fuel surcharge of +1% for every 5c diesel is above $1.70 (5% at $1.95), changed with one number when ZERA moves the price. Extras are separate lines: remote area (not on prepaid envelopes or phones), door collection or delivery, oversize, fragile, cover above $50, cash on delivery, Saturday. Then VAT 15.5%, rounded up to the next 50c because change is scarce.

Safeguards

No price jumps, no loopholes

The prices were tested with customers free to choose the cheapest option, and against edge cases, so no load or size can be sent for less by gaming the rules.

  • A whole vehicle never undercuts freight. It is offered from 3,000 kg, or when freight can't take the load, and never costs less than shared freight would charge for the same load. So 3,000 kg never costs less than 2,999 kg.
  • No jump where parcels end. Freight has an 80 kg minimum, so 71 kg costs about what 70 kg does.
  • Speed pays its way. Standard keeps today's price; Express carries the premium for next-day delivery, so cheaper services don't eat revenue.
  • Few shocks. Size counts only for big boxes, at most double the real weight in year one, so only 5–7% of shipments pay 30% or more extra, mostly people who choose Express and big, light boxes.
  • Nothing oversized slips through as a parcel. Parcels are at most 150 cm on any side; anything longer goes by freight at its minimum.
Decisions for ZimPost

Two choices only ZimPost can make

  • Are today's chart prices with VAT or without? We compared before VAT. If today's charts already include VAT, every base price here must drop by about 13% (÷ 1.155) so customers pay no more than now on average.
  • Freight against hiring a truck. Above about a tonne, per-kg freight costs 2 to 5 times what a whole truck costs to run (Harare → Mutare, 2 t: $1,116 freight against about $400 for the truck). Today's chart does the same. Capping freight at the truck cost would be fairer but cuts revenue by 20 to 28% on this month, so the proposal keeps today's freight level and leaves the cap as ZimPost's decision.
Rollout

How to switch without surprises

  • Counters first. Staff quote from the calculator; customers get a printed quote with each line. Today's charts stay visible for the first month for comparison.
  • Size pricing phased in. Year one: size counts for at most double the real weight. Review after six months of real data.
  • Fuel by the number. One fuel figure, reviewed when ZERA moves the diesel price, instead of reprinting the charts.
  • Watch it monthly. Revenue by service, route and branch against the same months last year, with the numbers adjusted in settings rather than in code.
  • Then online. The same calculator on WhatsApp and the web, then booking and tracking.
From ZimPost

What we need to confirm the numbers

  • 6 to 12 months of shipments (route, weight, service, price). The same comparison then runs on real data instead of the stand-in month.
  • The official list of items not accepted, and any rule for phones and medicine. The lists here follow DHL and other carriers and must be checked against ZimPost's own.
  • The phone price ZimPost wants. $5 is our proposal, at the EMS Pack 1 kg level.
  • Whether today's chart prices include VAT. The charts don't say.
  • Vehicle running costs, a road-distance table and the list of open outlets, to replace our estimates.
